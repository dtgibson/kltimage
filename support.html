Support

KLT Image is a free tool for exploring subtle image color structure while keeping the original unchanged.

Email developer@dtgibson.com for help.

Report a problem

Include your iOS or iPadOS version, KLT Image version and build, the action you were taking, image format and dimensions, and the exact error message. Share a sample photograph only if you have permission and are comfortable sharing its contents and metadata.

Opening images

Use a complete JPEG, PNG, TIFF, or HEIC original within the mobile limits of 26 million oriented pixels and 256 MiB of encoded source data. Allow a cloud-backed Photos or Files provider to obtain the original. A failed replacement keeps previously accepted work available.

Batch processing

Keep the app in the foreground and allow enough storage in your chosen destination. If a batch stops, completed files remain in its run folder. Review each item’s outcome and use the cleanup utility if it reports temporary data needing attention. Batches do not resume automatically, and existing outputs are not overwritten.

Interpreting results

Enhanced colors are exploratory visual evidence. Compare against the unchanged original and account for lighting, noise, compression, and clipping.

No account, subscription, or in-app purchase is required. Read the privacy policy for local file handling and system services.
