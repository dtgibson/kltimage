KLT Image for iPhone and iPad

Privacy policy

KLT Image collects no data and does not track you. Image processing happens locally on your device.

Local image processing

KLT Image has no developer-operated backend, accounts, advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs. It does not send your photographs, analysis records, saved methods, or usage activity to the developer.

The files you choose

Apple’s Photos picker provides only the items you select. Files and folder access use system pickers. If you choose a cloud-backed provider, that provider and the operating system handle transfers under their own policies.

Your current image, result, analysis record, and batch outcomes are held for the active session. Saved working spaces and transforms are stored in the app’s local library. Temporary files and cleanup reservations support safe importing, processing, exporting, and recovery; they do not form a photo history or a resumable batch queue.

The app checks local or specifically selected file metadata to validate inputs and avoid overwriting or removing another file. It checks available storage before writing. These values stay local and are not used for tracking.

Exporting and deleting

You choose when and where to export images, records, and method documents. Records can contain source filenames, image fingerprints, dimensions, and method details. Consider that information before sharing a record.

Delete saved methods in the library and manage exported files where you saved them. Deleting the app removes its app container according to iOS behavior. System or provider backups follow their own settings. There is no remote KLT Image account to delete.

System services and support

Apple’s App Store, TestFlight, diagnostics, and system services operate under Apple’s policies and your settings. This policy describes KLT Image’s behavior.

For support, email developer@dtgibson.com. You choose what to send, and your email provider handles the message. Do not include private photographs or metadata unless you intend to share them.

This website

These support and privacy pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. They contain no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or forms. GitHub processes website requests under its privacy statement. This website hosting is separate from the app’s local image processing.
